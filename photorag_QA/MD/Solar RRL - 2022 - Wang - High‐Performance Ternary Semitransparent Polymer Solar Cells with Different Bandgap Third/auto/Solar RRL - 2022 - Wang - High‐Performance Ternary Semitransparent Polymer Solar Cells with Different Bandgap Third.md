# High-Performance Ternary Semitransparent Polymer Solar Cells with Different Bandgap Third Component as Non-Fullerene Guest Acceptor

Xiangkun Wang, Xiaohua Zhai, Xiao Kang, Xiqiang Ding, Caiyun Gao, Xin Jing. Liangmin $\gamma _ { U }$ , and Mingliang Sun\*

Ternary semitransparent polymer solar cells (ST-PsCs) have great potential to simultaneously improve the power conversion efficiency (PCE) and average visible transmittance (AVT). Non-fullerene acceptor IHIC was synthesized by a simple one-step process under low cost and had an A-D-A structure similar to ITIC and IT-4F. Ternary device based on PM6:Y6:IHIC $( 2 0 \% )$ demonstrated higher hole and electron mobility (2.78, $3 . 7 2 \times 1 0 ^ { - 4 } \mathsf { c m } ^ { 2 } \mathsf { V } ^ { - 1 } \mathsf { S } ^ { - 1 } )$ and more balanced charge transport properties $( \mu _ { \mathrm { e } } / \mu _ { \mathrm { h } } = 1 . 1 2 )$ than the host binary device, and the ternary ST-PSC demonstrated a PCE of $1 2 . 1 8 \%$ and AVT of $2 7 . 0 7 \%$ -. Ternary PSCs with $20 \%$ ITIC or IT-4F also exhibited more balanced $\pmb { \mu _ { \mathrm { e } } } / \pmb { \mu _ { \mathrm { h } } }$ ratios (1.31 and 0.85), but with slightly enhanced short-current circuit density $( J { \boldsymbol { s } } { \boldsymbol { c } } )$ and reduced AVT $( 2 3 - 2 4 \% )$ , mainly owing to unavoidable absorption of third component in the visible region. Our conclusion in this work was that the little A-D-A non-fullerene acceptor could improve ternary blend film morphology and charge transport channels and enhanced $J _ { s c }$ and PCE, and PM6:Y6:IHIC $( 2 0 \% )$ with high AVT over $2 7 \%$ and PCE over $12 \%$ was more suitable in ST-PSCs than PM6:Y6:ITIC/IT-4F $( 2 0 \% )$ -.

# 1. Introduction

Polymer solar cells (PSCs) feature advantages of fexibility, semitransparency, light-weight, solution-processability.[1-6] Semitransparent PSCs (ST-PSCs)[7,8] have great potential in the real-time application of power windows, building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPVs), and so on.-H] Up to now, the power conversion efficiencies (PCEs) have reached $1 8 \%$ and $1 3 \%$ for opaque $\mathrm { P S C s } ^ { [ 1 2 - 1 4 ] }$ and ST-PSCs,15,16] respectively. The ternary strategy could improve light-harvest and short-circuit current densities $( J _ { \mathrm { S C } } ) , ^ { [ 1 7 - 1 9 ] }$ as well as open-circuit voltage $( V _ { \mathrm { O C } } )$ and fill factor (FF)[20,21] by incorporating the guest-acceptor into host-binary blends with well-complementary absorption and rationally varying proportion of host- and guestacceptor,[22-24] to further enhance photovoltaic performance.[19,25,26]

Ternary PSCs were fabricated originally based on two polymer donors and one fullerene accetor.27-29] Tenary systems also contained one polymer donor, one fullerene, and one non-fullerene acceptor since a set of non-fullerene acceptors was reported in 2015.130-33] Non-fullerene acceptors have multiple advantages, such as similar structure, energy-level adjustability, good compatibility, and broad absorption spectra. Nowadays, ternary PSCs based on one polymer donor and two non-fullerene acceptors have demonstrated increased PCEs of 16-19%.[13,14,16,19,34,35] It would be crucial that guest-acceptor exhibits compatibility and complementary absorption with host-acceptor in ternary ST-PSCs, where ternary blends could absorb NIR photons and pass partially visible photons. For example, Li et al.16] reported that the PBDB-TF: Y6:BTTPC-based ternary PSCs and ST-PSCs exhibited PCEs of $1 6 . 8 \%$ and $1 3 . 1 \%$ with an AVT of $2 2 . 4 \%$ respectively, by proper addition of BTTPC $( 1 0 \mathrm { w t \% } )$ as guestacceptor. Our group[36] in previous work had reported that the PM6:Y6:IT-M-based ternary ST-PSCs achieved optimal PCE of $1 1 . 6 7 \%$ and AVT of $2 2 . 2 0 \%$ , where ternary blend film with well-complementary absorption achieved a smooth surface morphology and a balanced charge transport compared to host-binary system.

In this work, PM6:Y6:IHIC ternary PSC demonstrated higher hole and electron mobility (2.78, $3 . 1 2 \times 1 0 ^ { - 4 } \mathrm { c m } ^ { 2 } \mathrm { V } ^ { - 1 } \mathrm { S } ^ { - \bar { 1 } } )$ and more balanced charge transport property $( \mu _ { \mathrm { e } } / \mu _ { \mathrm { h } } = 1 . 1 2 )$ than host binary PSC, which indicated that the incorporation of guest acceptor could improve charge transfer channel and efficiency. PM6:Y6:IHIC ternary ST-PSC demonstrated a PCE of $1 2 . 1 8 \%$ and AVT $2 7 . 0 7 \%$ ITIC and IT-4F were selected as third component and the $20 \%$ weight was incorporated into PM6:Y6 host binary blends, respectively. Due to the absorption of ITIC and IT-4 F located in the visible region, the incorporation of $20 \%$ ITIC/IT-4F may decrease device AVT. Two ternary system blend films also balance carrier mobility by enhancing hole mobility and decreasing electron mobility. The ratios of $\mu _ { \mathrm { e } } / \mu _ { \mathrm { h } }$ were 1.31 and 0.85 for PM6:Y6:ITIC and PM6:Y6:IT-4F, respectively, which were more balanced $( \approx 1 )$ than that of PM6:Y6. Comparing the three ternary device performances, we found that $2 0 \% \mathrm { I H I C } ^ { [ 9 , 3 9 ] }$ was the best choice in semitransparent ternary photovoltaic devices with high AVT over $2 7 \%$ and PCE over $12 \%$ . Even though the incorporation of $20 \%$ ITIC or IT-4F also improved blend film morphology and charge transportation, photocurrent density would be enhanced slightly and the average visible transmittance (AVT) was reduced $23 \mathrm { - } 2 4 \%$ AVT), which lowered that of PM6:Y6:IHIC, mainly owing to the $20 \%$ ITIC or IT-4F unavoidable absorption in the visible region. Therefore, our conclusion in this work was that the little A-D-A non-fullerene acceptor could improve ternary blend film morphology and charge transport channels and enhanced $J _ { \scriptscriptstyle \mathrm { S C } }$ and PCE, but PM6:Y6:IHIC was more suitable in ternary semitransparent devices than PM6:Y6:ITIC and PM6:Y6:IT-4 F.

Charge dissociation and extraction process of binary and ternary PsCs under optimal conditions were investigated by plotting photocurrent density $( J _ { \mathrm { p h } } )$ versus effective voltage $( V _ { \mathrm { e f f } } )$ shown in Figure 2c. According to $J _ { \mathrm { p h } } { = } J _ { \mathrm { L } } { - } J _ { \mathrm { D } } , J _ { \mathrm { L } }$ and $J _ { \mathrm { D } }$ are current densities under illumination and dark condition, respectively.[48] $V _ { \mathrm { e f f } } = V _ { 0 } - V ,$ where $V _ { 0 }$ is the voltage when $J _ { \mathrm { p h } } = 0$ and $V$ is the applied voltage. The $P _ { \mathrm { d i s s } }$ values were calculated as $9 9 . 1 \%$ $9 8 . 9 \%$ $9 8 . 3 \%$ for PM6:Y6:ITIC/IT-4F/IHIC-based ternary PSCs and $9 9 . 6 \%$ $9 7 . 6 \%$ $9 8 . 3 \%$ $9 5 . 9 9 \%$ for PM6:Y6/ITIC/ IT-4F/IHIC-based binary PSCs, respectively, where $P _ { \mathrm { d i s s } } = J _ { \mathrm { p h } } /$ $J _ { \mathrm { s a t } } ,$ and $J _ { \mathrm { s a t } }$ is saturation current density. The conclusion demonstrates non-fullerene guest-acceptors have no negative effects and promote charge generation and exciton dissociation in active layers of ternary devices. The charge recombination process could also directly affect the device performance, as well as the exciton dissociation process. To explore the charge recombination kinetics, the dependence of $J _ { \scriptscriptstyle \mathrm { S C } }$ versus varied light intensity $( P _ { \mathrm { l i g h t } } )$ were plotted, following the power-law relationship $J _ { \scriptscriptstyle \mathrm { S C } }$ $\propto P \alpha$ light, 44,49,50] where the $\alpha$ could be calculated and obtained from the slope of the fitting curve $\log ( J _ { \mathrm { S C } } ) { - } \log ( P _ { \mathrm { l i g h t } } )$ [51] The $\alpha$ value approaching 1 indicates that bimolecular recombination is weakened and negligible, therefore enhanced FF and $J _ { \scriptscriptstyle \mathrm { S C } }$ in PSCs are obtained. As illustrated in Figure 2d, the $\alpha$ values were calculated as 0.99, 0.99, 0.98 for PM6:Y6:ITIC/IT-4F/IHIC-based ternary PSCs and 0.98, 0.93, 0.94, 0.97 for PM6:Y6/ITIC/IT-4F/ IHIC-based binary PSCs, respectively, which indicate low bimolecular recombination in ternary photovoltaic devices.

# 2.3. Photovoltaic Performance of Semitransparent Ternary Devices

Semitransparent photovoltaic devices!52-55] were fabricated based on PM6:Y6:ITIC/IT-4 F/IHIC-based ternary systems under optimal conditions via lowering the thickness of the cathode electrode. Ternary semitransparent strategy[56-58] could achieve the trade-off among photovoltaic performance,59] absorption property, and transmittance. In this work, ST-PSCs were fabricated with a device structure of $\mathrm { I T O } /$ PEDOT:PSS/ PM6:Y6:ITIC/ IT-4 F/IHIC (1.0:1.0:0.2, w/w/w)/ PDINO/ Au $( 1 \mathrm { n m } )$ and $\mathrm { A g }$ $( 1 0 / 1 5 / 2 0 \mathrm { n m } )$ . # 2.4. Photoluminescence and Morphology Characteristics

To further explore the charge transport and energy transfer among donor PM6, host- and guest-acceptors, the steady-state photoluminescence (PL) was measured. Obviously, the emission spectra of ITIC/IT-4F were overlapped absolutely and IHIC was overlapped partly by the absorption spectra of Y6, shown in Figure S8a, Supporting Information, demonstrating the existence of efficient energy transfer from guest-acceptor to Y6 according to the Forster theory.[62] The PL emission peaks of ITIC at $7 7 0 \mathrm { n m }$ , IT-4F at $8 0 0 \mathrm { n m }$ , and IHIC at $8 6 0 \mathrm { n m }$ were completely quenched in blend films, shown in Figure S8b, Supporting Information, while the emission peak of Y6:ITIC/IT-4F/IHIC blends obviously strengthened at $9 1 0 \mathrm { n m }$ demonstrating the potential energy transfer between Y6 and guest-acceptors.[63] Subsequently, the PL quenching effect was investigated to clarify the exciton dissociation dynamics in binary and ternary blends as shown in Figure S8c, Supporting Information. The intense emission peak of PM6 was quenched completely at $7 0 0 \mathrm { n m }$ in binary and ternary blend systems, suggesting the excitons could be efficiently dissociated by acceptor at interfaces of donor and acceptor domains.64] In addition, the acceptor-only devices were fabricated with a conventional structure to exclude the possibility of charge transport between host- and guest-acceptors. The $J { - } V$ characteristics based on the Y6, ITIC, Y6:ITIC, IT-4F, Y6:IT-4F, IHIC, Y6:IHIC were shown in Figure S8d-f, Supporting Information. The Js $_ { \textrm { C } } ( 0 . 2 7 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 }$ for Y6:ITIC) was between that of Y6 $( 0 . 1 0 \mathrm { m A } \mathrm { c m } ^ { - 2 } )$ and ITIC $( 0 . 7 7 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 } )$ the $J _ { \scriptscriptstyle \mathrm { S C } }$ $( 0 . 0 9 8 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 }$ for Y6:IT-4F) was between that of Y6 $( 0 . 1 0 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 } )$ and IT-4F $( 0 . 0 9 5 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 } )$ ,and the $J _ { \mathrm { S C } }$ $( 0 . 1 0 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 }$ for Y6:IHIC) was between that of Y6 $( 0 . 1 1 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 } )$ and IHIC $( 0 . 0 9 7 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 } )$ ,indicating negligible charge transport between Y6 and ITIC/IT-4F/IHIC, as shown in Figure S8g, Supporting Information.

The contact-angle measurements were applied to investigate the miscibility of non-fullerene guest-acceptors and Y6 via the Flory-Huggins model.[16,65] The contact-angle parameters were shown in Figure S9 and Table S5, Supporting Information, where surface free energy (SFE, y) values of ITIC, IT-4 F, IHIC, Y6, and PM6 were 44.43, 39.66, 43.70, 39.41, and $4 7 . 3 3 \mathrm { m J } \mathrm { c m } ^ { - 2 }$ under neat film condition, respectively. # 3. Conclusion

In summary, IHIC had an A-D-A structure and was synthesized by a simple one-step process at low cost. PM6:Y6:IHIC ternary PSC demonstrated higher hole and electron mobility (2.78, $3 . 1 2 \times 1 0 ^ { - 4 } \mathrm { c m } ^ { 2 } \mathrm { V } ^ { - 1 } \mathrm { S } ^ { - 1 } )$ and more balanced charge transport property $( \mu _ { \mathrm { e } } / \mu _ { \mathrm { h } } = 1 . 1 2$ than host binary PSC. PM6:Y6:IHIC ternary ST-PSC demonstrated a PCE of $1 2 . 1 8 \%$ and an AVT $2 7 . 0 7 \%$ . Then PM6:Y6:ITIC and PM6:Y6:IT-4F also exhibited more balanced $\mu _ { \mathrm { e } } / \mu _ { \mathrm { h } }$ ratios (1.31 and 0.85) than that of PM6: Y6. Comparing three ternary semitransparent devices, we found that $2 0 \% \mathrm { I H I C }$ was the best choice in semitransparent ternary photovoltaic devices with high AVT over $2 7 \%$ and PCE over $12 \%$ Even though the incorporation of $20 \%$ ITIC or IT-4F also improved blend film morphology and charge transportation, photocurrent density would be enhanced slightly and the AVT was reduced $23 \mathrm { - } 2 4 \%$ AVT), which lowered that of PM6:Y6: IHIC, mainly owing to unavoidable absorption of the $20 \%$ ITIC or IT-4F in the visible region. Therefore, our conclusion in this work was that the little A-D-A non-fullerene acceptor could improve ternary blend film morphology and charge transport channels and enhanced $J _ { \scriptscriptstyle \mathrm { S C } }$ and PCE, but PM6:Y6:IHIC was more suitable in ternary semitransparent devices than PM6: Y6:ITIC and PM6:Y6:IT-4F.
