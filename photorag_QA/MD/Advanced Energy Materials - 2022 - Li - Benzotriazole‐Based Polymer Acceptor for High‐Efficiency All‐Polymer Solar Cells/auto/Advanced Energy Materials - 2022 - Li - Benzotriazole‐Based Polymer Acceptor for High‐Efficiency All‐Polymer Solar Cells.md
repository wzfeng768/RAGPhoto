# Benzotriazole-Based Polymer Acceptor for High-Efficiency All-Polymer Solar Cells with High Photocurrent and Low Voltage Loss

Xiaoming Li, Xiaopeng Duan, Jiawei Qiao, Shilin Li, Yunhao Cai, Jianqi Zhang, Yuan Zhang, Xiaotao Hao,\* and Yanming Sun\*

The power conversion efficiencies (PCEs) of all-polymer solar cells (all-PSCs) have already exceeded $1 7 \%$ . However, the limited absorption range of an allpolymer system results in significantly reduced short-circuit current density $\left( \boldsymbol { J } _ { s c } \right)$ , which eventually influences the PCE improvement. To broaden the light absorption of polymer acceptors, herein, benzotriazole is introduced in the core unit of small molecule acceptors and thus two narrow-bandgap polymer acceptors named PTz-BO and PTz-C11 featuring the same molecular backbone and different side-chain length are synthesized. Compared with PTz-C11, the PTz-BO based-all PSCs deliver a slightly reduced $J _ { s c }$ a large open-circuit voltage $( \boldsymbol { v } _ { \mathrm { o c } } )$ and a low voltage loss below $\phantom { - } 0 . 5 0 1$ Moreover, ternary all-PSCs are constructed by introducing PTz-C11 as a guest component. Benefiting from the reduced recombination, improved exciton generation and dissociation, and balanced charge transport, a high efficiency of $1 6 . 5 8 \%$ is obtained for the ternary all-PSCs, with a high $J _ { s c }$ over $2 5 \mathsf { m A c m } ^ { - 2 }$ without sacrificing the $\pmb { V _ { \mathrm { o c } } }$ . Such result represents the highest efficiency reported for benzotriazole-based all-PsCs in the literature thus far. This work demonstrates the great potential of benzotriazole for the synthesis of efficient narrow-bandgap polymer acceptors.

# 1. Introduction

Polymer solar cells (PsCs) have been regarded as one of the most promising next-generation clean and renewable energies.[1-3] In the past few years, thanks to the emergence of small molecule acceptors (SMAs), the power conversion efficiencies (PCEs) of PSCs have exceeded $1 9 \%$ [4-7] Nevertheless, to realize the commercialization of psCs, the flexibility, operation stability, mechanical properties, and thermal stress still need to be further improved. Notably, all-polymer solar cells (all-PSCs), which are composed of both polymer donor and polymer acceptor, are more feasible for the practical application since they possess superior thermal and mechanical robustness compared to polymer/SMA systems.[8-11] However, the state-of-the-art all-PSCs encounter inferior PCEs than those of polymer/SMA counterparts.[12-19] Therefore, the innovation of high-performance polymer acceptors to improve the PCEs of all-PSCs is urgently needed.

In the early stage of polymer acceptor development, perylene diimide, naphthalene diimide, and boronnitrogen coordination units have been widely explored.20-23 Imputing to the deficient light-harnessing or low electron mobility of these polymer acceptors, the PCEs of all-PSCs were impeded seriously. Until 2017, a valid strategy of polymerizing SMA was proposed, which could preserve the benefits of high electron affinity and better light harvest in non-fullerene acceptors well.24] Subsequently, this strategy has been frequently used in the design and synthesis of polymer acceptors. Among them, due to their extraordinary performance in SMA-based devices, Y6 and its derivatives have been extensively employed as the building blocks to achieve the efficiency breakthrough of all-PSCs.[25-28] Currently, most of the high-performance all-PSCs are fabricated with benzo[c][1,2,5]thiadiazole (BT)-core fused SMAs, which have restricted absorption range and thus results in unsatisfied photocurrent.29,30] In comparison with BT-containing polymer acceptors, benzotriazole-based polymer acceptors could maintain high-lying lowest unoccupied molecular orbitals (LUMO) energy levels and achieve significantly redshifted light absorption due to relatively weak electron-withdraw capability of benzotriazole unit.31,32] Moreover, benzotriazole-based conjugated molecules usually have high photoluminescence quantum yield, which could enable an efficient radiative recombination pathway and enhance the electroluminescence yield.33-35] However, the successful design of benzotriazole-based polymer acceptors is rare, and the voltage loss is not low enough, which cannot ensure large open-circuit voltage $( V _ { \mathrm { o c } } )$ and high shortcircuit current density $( J _ { \mathrm { s c } } )$ in devices simultaneously.

Herein, we designed and synthesized two narrow-bandgap polymer acceptors named PTz-BO and PTz-C11 by introducing benzotriazole into the central core with different alkyl side chains in the beta position of the thiophene unit, respectively. As expected, after introducing benzotriazole-core to replace BT-core in the polymer backbone, the resulting two polymers display significantly redshifted optical absorption and upshifted energy levels. Compared to PTz-C11, PTz-BO shows slightly blueshifted absorption profile with higher absorption coefficient as well as LUMO energy level. When blended with the polymer donor, PBDB-T, the PTz-BO and PTz-C11-based all-PSCs exhibit PCEs of $1 5 . 7 6 \%$ and $1 5 . 5 9 \%$ ,respectively. Impressively, a low voltage loss of $0 . 4 9 8 \mathrm { ~ V ~ }$ is obtained in the PBDB-T:PTz-BO devices. Moreover, all-PSCs were constructed by inviting PTz-C11 as a third component into PBDB-T:PTz-BO binary blend. Encouragingly, the ternary device exhibits higher $J _ { \mathrm { s c } }$ and $V _ { \mathrm { o c } }$ simultaneously, thus an outstanding efficiency of $1 6 . 5 8 \%$ is eventually achieved, representing the highest efficiency for benzotriazole polymer acceptor-based all-PSCs so far.

# 2. Results and Discussion

The chemical structures of PBDB-T, PTz-BO, and PTz-C11 are shown in Figure 1a. The synthetic procedures of polymer acceptors are depicted in Scheme S1, Supporting Information.

Compound 1 was synthesized by Stille coupling between 4,7-dibromo-2-(2-ethylhexyl)-5,6-dinitro-2H-benzo[d][1,2,3] triazole and tributyl(6-(2-butyloctyl)thieno[3,2-b]thiophen-2-yl) stannane. Then, compound 1 occurred the double intramolecular Cadogan reductive cyclization to obtain the benzotriazolecontaining ladder-type fused ring backbone. After that, 11-(bromomethyl)tricosane was introduced to the nitrogen atoms of the pentacyclic units under alkaline condition to improve the solubility of the resultant polymer acceptors. Subsequently, Compound 2 was subjected to Vilsmeier-Haack reaction to obtain target product Compound 3. The dialdehyde Compound 3 conducted a condensation reaction with highpurity IC-Br-yto prepare the monomer TPBN-BO. The TPBNC11 monomer was produced according to the literature.2] Finally, the regioregular copolymers of PTz-BO, and $\mathrm { P T z }$ -C11 were available via the palladium-catalyzed Stille polycondensation between the monomers and 2,5-bis(trimethylstannanyl) thiophene. High-temperature gel permeation chromatography measurement shows that PTz-BO has a molecular weight of $1 6 . 7 \mathrm { k D a }$ and polydispersity of 3.0, respectively.

The UV-vis absorption spectra of the PTz-BO and $\mathrm { P T z }$ -C11 were measured in both diluted chloroform solution and neat films. As displayed in Figure 1b and Figure S1, Supporting Information, significant redshifted absorption spectra were observed from the solution to the solid-state, and the main absorption peaks are located at $8 3 4 ~ \mathrm { n m }$ . Interestingly, PTz-C11 film exhibits a wide absorption wavelength range, which is attributed to the more delocalized LUMO in this polymer. To explore the origin of the high $J _ { \mathrm { s c } }$ for PTz series-based allPSCs, we first investigated the exciton dissociation efficiency by performing steady-state photoluminescence (PL). From the steady-state PL spectra of the neat and blend films (Figure S3a,b, Supporting Information), we can find that PBDB-T:PTz-BO blend exhibits a higher PL quenching efficiency compared with PBDB-T:PTz-C11 blend. When the third component $\mathrm { P T z }$ C11 was added into PBDB-T:PTz-BO blend, the ternary blend exhibits a higher quenching efficiency, suggesting the most efficient exciton dissociation and potentially obtaining a high $J _ { \mathrm { s c } }$ Furthermore, the time-resolved transient photoluminescence spectra were measured to understand the dynamics of charge transfer with the binary and ternary blends. As presented in Figure S3c, Supporting Information, the average fluorescence lifetime ( $\varTheta$ of blend film PBDB-T:PTz-C11 (58.15 ps) is slightly longer than that of blend film PBDB-T:PTz-BO (571 ps). As for the ternary blend, the significantly depressed $\tau$ (47.9 ps) is indicative of faster exciton dynamics, implying the more efficient charge transfer in the ternary device.

Furthermore, the exciton dissociation behaviors of binary all-PSCs and ternary all-PsCs were studied by measuring the photocurrent density $\left( J _ { \mathrm { p h } } \right)$ versus effective voltage $( V _ { \mathrm { e f f } } )$ (Figure 2e). $J _ { \mathrm { p h } }$ is defined as $\bar { J } _ { \mathrm { p h } } = J _ { \mathrm { L } } - J _ { \mathrm { D } }$ where $J _ { \mathrm { L } }$ and $J _ { \mathrm { D } }$ represent the current densities in the illuminated and dark, respectively. $V _ { \mathrm { e f f } }$ is expressed by $V _ { \mathrm { e f f } } = V _ { 0 } - V _ { \mathrm { a } }$ where $V _ { 0 }$ is the voltage at which $J _ { \mathrm { p h } }$ is zero, and $V _ { \mathrm { a } }$ is the applied bias voltage.[38,39] The exciton dissociation probability $( P _ { \mathrm { d i s s } } )$ was accessed by $J _ { \mathrm { p h } } / J _ { \mathrm { s a t } }$ under the short-circuit condition, which was calculated to be $9 5 . 8 \%$ $9 4 . 3 \%$ , and $9 6 . 1 \%$ for PBDB-T:PTz-BO, PBDB$\mathrm { T } \mathrm { : P T z }$ -C11, and PBDB-T:PTz-BO:PTz-C11 devices, respectively. These results indicate that the BO-substitution can facilitate the exciton dissociation and charge carrier generation in the all-PsCs. Moreover, the introduction of the third component enables the above processes occur more efficiently in ternary devices, as compared with those binary devices. Then, the charge recombination behaviors for these devices were examined by performing the dependence of $V _ { \mathrm { o c } }$ on the light intensity. As presented in Figure 2f, for the binary device, the slopes of the $V _ { \mathrm { o c } }$ versus $P _ { \mathrm { l i g h t } }$ curves of the PTz-BO and $\mathrm { P T z }$ C11-processed devices are 1.19 $\bar { k } \mathrm { T } / q$ and $1 . 2 1 \ k \mathrm { T } / q$ , respectively, where k is the Boltzmann constant, $\mathrm { T }$ is the absolute temperature in Kelvin, and q is the elementary charge. Furthermore, the ternary device shows a lower slope value of 1.11 $k \mathrm { T / q }$ indicating that PTz-C11 indeed leads to reduced trap-assisted recombination in the ternary blend, which contributes to the improved FF and PCE.

To investigate the charge transport properties of these devices, we measured the hole $( \mu _ { \mathrm { h } } )$ and electron $( \mu _ { \mathrm { e } } )$ mobilities of the neat films of polymer acceptors and PBDB-T:PTz-C11, PBDBT:PTz-BO, and PBDB-T:PTz-BO:PTz-C11 blends by using the space charge limited current method.(40,41] The hole-only devices and electron-only devices are comprised of ITO/PEDOT:PSS/ active layers $\mathrm { { ' M o O } _ { 3 } / A g }$ and ITO/ZnO/active layers/PNDIT$\mathrm { F } 3 \mathrm { N } / \mathrm { A g }$ respectively. The average $\mu _ { \mathrm { e } }$ value of PTz-C11 neat film is slightly higher than that of PTz-BO neat film (Figure S4 and Table S4). The average electron/hole mobilities of the PBDB-T:PTz-BO, PBDB-T:PTz-C11, and PBDB-T:PTz-BO:PTzC11 blends are $3 . 3 4 \times 1 0 ^ { - 4 } / 2 . 2 6 \times 1 0 ^ { - 4 }$ $3 . 7 1 \times 1 0 ^ { - 4 } / 2 . 2 2 \times 1 0 ^ { - 4 }$ and $3 . 0 2 \times 1 0 ^ { - 4 } / 2 . 2 4 \times 1 0 ^ { - 4 } ~ \mathrm { c m } ^ { 2 } ~ \mathrm { V } ^ { - 1 } ~ \mathrm { s } ^ { - 1 }$ which correspond to $\mu _ { \mathrm { e } } / \mu _ { \mathrm { h } }$ ratios of 1.48, 1.67, and 1.35, respectively. For the ternary blend, both the hole and electron mobilities slightly decreased, which could be attributed to attenuated molecular packing of blend film, agreeing well with the grazing-incidence wide-angle X-ray scattering (GIWAXs) results. A more balanced charge carrier mobility in the ternary blend is conducive to charge transport, thus leading to high FF.[42]

Subsequently, the contact angle measurement was performed to study the compatibility between PBDB-T and polymer acceptors based on liquid drops of water and glycerin (Figure S5, Supporting Information). According to Wu's model,[44] the surface tensions $( \gamma$ of different materials were calculated to be 22.3, 25.7, and $2 8 . 2 \mathrm { m N } \mathrm { m } ^ { - 1 }$ for PBDB-T, PTz-BO, and PTz-C11, respectively. The corresponding molecular miscibility can be estimated by Flory-Huggins interaction parameter $\chi ,$ whose equation is $\dot { \chi } \propto ( \sqrt { \gamma _ { A } } - \sqrt { \gamma _ { B } } ) ^ { 2 }$ . The evaluated $\chi$ values between PBDB-T/ PTz-BO and PBDB-T/PTz-C11 are O.121 and $0 . 3 4 6 ~ \mathrm { \ m N ~ m ^ { - 1 } }$ respectively. The high degree of miscibility of the two acceptors could lead to a desirable morphology and molecular ordering in the ternary blend and further result in a better performance.

# 3. Conclusion

In conclusion, we designed and synthesized two efficient polymer acceptors, $\mathrm { P T z } { \cdot } \mathrm { B O }$ and PTz-C11. Compared with $\mathrm { P T z }$ C11, PTz-BO shows a higher absorption coefficient and upshifted LUMO energy level. When blended with PBDB-T, PTz-BO-based all-PSCs show higher $\mathrm { E Q E _ { E L } }$ and a lower $V _ { \mathrm { l o s s } }$ than the PTz-C11- based devices. Impressively, as a guest component, PTz-C11 can not only provide a broader absorption window, but also act as a phase mediator. Reduced non-radiative recombination, improved exciton separation, suppressed trap-assisted recombination, and balanced charge transport are achieved in the ternary device. As a result, the corresponding devices yield a PCE of $1 6 . 5 8 \%$ with a high $J _ { \mathrm { s c } }$ over $2 5 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ , and meanwhile a low $V _ { \mathrm { l o s s } }$ below $0 . 5 0 ~ \mathrm { V }$ is achieved. Our work provides an effective molecular design strategy to synthesize high-performance benzotriazolebased polymer acceptors, which is expected to address the tradeoff issue between photocurrent and voltage in all-PSCs.
